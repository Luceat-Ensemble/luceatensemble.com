Privacy

Who is responsible

Associazione Luceat, 6600 Locarno, luceatensemble@outlook.com.

What data we collect and why

With the "Friends of Luceat" form we collect first name, last name, email address, the chosen contribution and consent to the newsletter. We use them only to manage your membership, send you your free tickets and, if you wish, the newsletter. We do not sell them or pass them on to third parties for other purposes.

Where it is stored

The website is hosted by Netlify (USA). The "Friends of Luceat" form and the newsletter are managed by EmailOctopus, which stores the form data; to protect the form from automated submissions EmailOctopus uses Google reCAPTCHA. These providers may process data abroad; we chose them because they offer adequate data protection.

Cookies and statistics

The website uses no cookies of its own and no analytics. On the "Friends of Luceat" page the EmailOctopus form and Google reCAPTCHA may use technical cookies. YouTube videos load only when you press play; from then on YouTube's (Google's) privacy policy applies.

Your rights

You can ask at any time what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and unsubscribe from the newsletter by writing to luceatensemble@outlook.com.
